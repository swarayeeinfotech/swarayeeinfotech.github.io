About Swarayee

Education • Technology • Digital Services • Business Support

Swarayee Comps & Infotech

Swarayee Comps & Infotech is focused on providing practical computer education, skill development and digital business support services.

Our objective is to connect students, job seekers, entrepreneurs and rural communities with useful technology and professional services.

Through our centres, we provide computer courses, online services, banking support, printing services, courier services and business-related solutions.
